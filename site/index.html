A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Does Iyal replace professional advice?+

No. Iyal is non-medical design assistance. It does not diagnose or treat conditions. Some adaptations need a qualified professional to review fit, setup or manufacturing before use.

Can I ask directly for a custom design?+

Yes. You can explicitly request custom design rather than an existing-product comparison. Current automated CAD supports grip sleeves; measurements and engineering checks still apply.

Is a generated design ready to manufacture?+

It is a prototype. Passing numeric design checks does not establish physical fit, comfort or manufacturing safety. Materials, dimensions and actual use need review and testing.

Is this the live Iyal application?+

This page introduces Iyal and helps you prepare a conversation. The live application opens when you choose “Talk to Iyal” or “Continue with Iyal”. That is where you can share photos or video, record your voice, and generate supported grip-sleeve prototypes.
